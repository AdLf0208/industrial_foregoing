# `assets/` and `data/` — what is carried and what is written

Since the 2026-11-18 SDK migration these two folders sit at the port root and travel inside the one
flat `industrialforegoing.hmod`, beside `src/` (the manifest is `horizon-mod.toml` at the root, not a
content-only one). The paths below are the package's.

Generated by [`tools/assemble_content.py`](tools/assemble_content.py) from the
ATM10 jar `ports/_shared/licence-cache/industrialforegoing-1.21-3.6.39.jar`
(SHA-1 `a653f5c2…77fd` = the pack record; provenance in
[`../_shared/carry-work/industrialforegoing/SOURCE.md`](../_shared/carry-work/industrialforegoing/SOURCE.md)).
Rerun the script to rebuild; it deletes `assets/` and `data/` first.

## Deliberately not carried

- The **GUI texture** and the menu: the machine has no item slots and upstream's
  screen is Titanium's tank/energy/progress bars; slice 1 exposes the same
  numbers through `/industrialforegoing info` instead (gap 1 in `notes.md`).
- The **`industrialforegoing:machine_frame/pity`** tag and the **`c:plastics`**
  tag: their items (`machine_frame_pity`, `plastic`) belong to other parts of
  the mod, so the carried recipe cannot match until those are ported — recorded
  as gap 6.
- Every other blockstate/model/texture in the jar (72 blockstates, 153 block
  models, 146 block textures): other machines, frames, mycelial generators — the
  rest of the mod.
- `pack.mcmeta` and the jar's `META-INF`: not content.
